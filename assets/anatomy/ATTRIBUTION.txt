Human Atlas adaptations: units/axes converted, geometry simplified with
meshoptimizer (0.2% relative error), normals quantized, binary packaging.

MacroTracker adaptations: selected back/shoulder structures, torso cropping,
surface subdivision and smooth shading, neutral context materials, thin
trapezius center seam, workout group/region metadata, Blender/glTF export.
Source IDs are preserved in glTF extras and the editable source manifest.
No endorsement by the original creators is implied.

Full-body model adaptations (October 7, 2026): selected limb, chest, gluteal,
back and neck anatomy; smoothing and mesh simplification; shared continuous
trapezius from the approved preview; neutral head/hands/feet and interior shell;
closed medial chest spacing; lateral external-oblique sheet trimmed to expose
the rectus region; neutral pelvis without external genital details.

Latissimus, abdominal training regions, multifidus, quadratus lumborum and the
internal-oblique sheet are schematic adaptations fitted to the source envelope,
not original named source meshes. Gluteus medius/minimus posterior and lateral
portions represent the app's separate glute/abductor accounting groups, not
additional anatomical muscles. Whole and head views share the same allocation
data. The complete model loads one regional GLB and recolors by group as needed.
